# Office Financing Fit

Identify likely lender categories, debt sizing constraints, reserve requirements, and financing risks for an office asset.

---

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when evaluating acquisition debt, refinance options, bridge financing, rescue capital, or loan maturity risk for an office building.

---

## What You'll Need to Provide

- Property profile, location, rentable area, asset quality, and occupancy
- Rent roll with tenant concentration and rollover
- NOI, T-12, budget, and stabilized pro forma
- Leasing cost and capital plan
- Existing debt terms if refinancing
- Requested loan amount, basis, purchase price, or payoff
- Sponsor balance sheet and liquidity context if available

---

## Mission

Determine which lender lanes are realistic and what likely constrains proceeds: DSCR, debt yield, LTV, tenant risk, rollover, reserves, sponsor liquidity, or capital-market appetite.

---

## Strategy

### Step 1: Classify Asset Financeability

Classify:

- CORE FINANCEABLE: high-quality, stabilized, strong tenancy, low rollover
- SELECTIVE: decent asset but office risk, moderate rollover, needs relationship lender
- TRANSITIONAL: vacancy, lease-up, heavy TI/LC, repositioning capital
- DISTRESSED / SPECIAL SITUATION: maturity default, impaired value, heavy vacancy, uncertain plan

### Step 2: Size Debt by Multiple Tests

Calculate or request:

- in-place NOI DSCR
- stabilized NOI DSCR
- debt yield
- LTV
- loan per RSF
- debt service through lease-up
- leasing-cost reserve
- capex reserve
- refinance test at maturity

### Step 3: Match Lender Lanes

Use Office Lender Criteria to identify:

- relationship bank
- life company
- CMBS
- debt fund / bridge
- SBA / owner-user
- private credit / rescue capital

Explain why each is likely, unlikely, or conditional.

### Step 4: Identify Required Structure

Recommend:

- upfront reserve
- future funding
- earnout
- cash sweep
- leasing milestone
- recourse / partial recourse
- amortization vs interest-only
- extension tests
- tenant notice covenants

### Step 5: State Financing Verdict

Provide a practical verdict:

- FINANCEABLE NOW
- FINANCEABLE WITH STRUCTURE
- BRIDGE / RESCUE ONLY
- NOT FINANCEABLE AT REQUESTED PROCEEDS

---

## Quality Checks

- Uses in-place and stabilized NOI separately
- Discusses rollover and tenant concentration
- Includes TI/LC and capex reserve needs
- Does not assume CMBS or life company execution for transitional office without support
- Identifies the likely controlling sizing test
- States what data is required for real quotes

---

## Red Flags & Dealbreakers

- In-place NOI fails coverage and sponsor has no reserve plan
- Major rollover occurs before loan maturity
- Requested loan amount exceeds supportable proceeds under multiple tests
- Financing plan assumes a refinance before stabilization
- Borrower cannot fund leasing costs
- Debt type mismatches asset plan, such as fixed-rate CMBS for active repositioning

---

## When Data is Missing

- If debt terms are missing, show sizing ranges rather than a false quote
- If tenant rollover is missing, classify proceeds as preliminary only
- If capex or TI/LC plan is missing, require reserves before calling the deal financeable
- If sponsor liquidity is missing, flag lender appetite as uncertain

---

## Confidence Scoring

| Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| HIGH | Rent roll, NOI, debt request, capex plan, leasing plan, and sponsor context are available |
| MEDIUM | Property and NOI data available, but sponsor liquidity or leasing reserve detail is incomplete |
| LOW | Summary property data only; no reliable rollover or cash-flow detail |
