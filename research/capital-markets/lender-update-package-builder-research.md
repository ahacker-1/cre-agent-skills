## Key Findings

- A lender package should lead with the ask, then prove asset performance and recovery logic.
- NOI bridge, business-plan milestones, and sponsor contribution are core credibility tools.
- Exhibit readiness matters as much as narrative.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require executive summary, NOI bridge, exhibit checklist, lender protections, and timeline.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- Package emphasis should vary by audience: relationship lender, servicer, debt fund, or rescue-capital investor.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Owner narrative conflicts with financial exhibits.
- Requested decision is unclear or requires unconfirmed consent.

## Open Questions

- Each lender may require its own forms, reporting templates, and consent package.
