## Key Findings

- IC memo should compare alternatives, not merely justify the sponsor's preferred path.
- Recommendation must separate value preservation from ownership preservation.
- Conditions to approval are necessary where lender consent, legal review, tax impact, or term sheets are incomplete.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require recommendation status: approve, approve with conditions, defer, or reject.
- Require alternatives table and 30/60/90 day plan.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- Deal-specific outputs from prior skills control over general market context.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Rescue capital preserves asset but leaves no return for existing equity.
- Sale is rejected without a credible refinance or recap path.

## Open Questions

- IC decision may require separate legal, tax, appraisal, and lender quote support.
