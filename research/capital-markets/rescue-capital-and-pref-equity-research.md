## Key Findings

- Preferred equity can bridge maturity and refinance gaps, but it reprices common equity through priority returns, governance, and dilution.
- Mezzanine debt is not interchangeable with preferred equity because remedies and senior-lender consent mechanics differ.
- Rescue capital is appropriate only when there is a credible stabilization or exit path.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require comparison across cost, certainty, control, dilution, consent, and exit feasibility.
- Require sponsor-incentive analysis to avoid over-diluting the operating sponsor.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- Law-firm sources are used for structure and issue spotting, not legal advice.
- Promotional capital-provider sources are treated as examples, not benchmarks.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Preferred equity with hard current pay can be more burdensome than debt.
- Rescue capital can preserve ownership while destroying residual economics.

## Open Questions

- Live preferred-equity returns, recognition terms, and control rights require transaction-specific term sheets.
