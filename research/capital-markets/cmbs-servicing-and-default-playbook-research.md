- Supports `knowledge/cmbs-servicing-and-default-playbook.md`

## U.S.-Only Assumptions

- Geography: United States
- Deal type: securitized commercial mortgage loans and related servicing interactions
- Document rule: PSA and loan documents control

## Key Findings

- CMBS outcomes depend on PSA authority, servicing status, and recovery-maximization standards.
- Borrower readiness requires current financials, valuation, business plan, legal issues, and a specific ask.
- Transfer to special servicing changes process, incentives, costs, and timeline.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require servicing map and package-readiness checklist.
- Require recovery comparison for material borrower requests.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- CREFC materials define structure; Trepp and practitioner sources explain market process.
- Actual PSA and loan documents override general playbook.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Borrower sends unsupported requests to the wrong servicer.
- Default communication occurs without counsel review.

## Open Questions

- Each trust's PSA, controlling class, and servicing standard must be confirmed from documents.
