## Key Findings

- Borrower readiness depends on current financial package, specific ask, recovery story, and servicing map.
- Master servicer and special servicer authority differ.
- Special-servicer decisions are evaluated through recovery and PSA constraints, not borrower preference alone.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require servicing map, transfer/default risk table, and package-readiness checklist.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- PSA and loan documents override generalized playbook.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Borrower contacts wrong party with a material modification request.
- Borrower communicates default risk without counsel strategy.

## Open Questions

- Trust-specific controlling class and consent rights must be confirmed from documents.
