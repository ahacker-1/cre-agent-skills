## Key Findings

- The strongest workout proposals pair borrower relief with lender protections.
- Extensions should be tied to measurable milestones.
- CMBS loans require special attention to servicing authority and recovery standards.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require structure comparison table and lender give/get logic.
- Require timeline with evidence for each milestone.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- Balance-sheet lenders may negotiate flexibly; CMBS outcomes are PSA constrained.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Relief requested after liquidity has already run out.
- Borrower asks for time but does not show a funded plan.

## Open Questions

- Actual default waiver, release, guaranty, and tax effects require counsel.
