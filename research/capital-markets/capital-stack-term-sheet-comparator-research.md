## Key Findings

- Term sheets must be compared on net proceeds, not headline proceeds.
- Capital cost includes current pay, accrual, fees, exit costs, dilution, and control rights.
- Lowest rate can be the wrong choice if reserves, recourse, or control terms are punitive.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require normalized term table and weighted scorecard.
- Require hidden-issue section for recourse, sweeps, consent rights, lockouts, and forced-sale rights.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- If sponsor objective conflicts with lowest cost, rank by objective and disclose tradeoff.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Highest proceeds proposal has insufficient net proceeds after reserves.
- Preferred equity term looks cheaper until redemption or control provisions are modeled.

## Open Questions

- Full legal review is needed for recourse, intercreditor, recognition, and transfer provisions.
