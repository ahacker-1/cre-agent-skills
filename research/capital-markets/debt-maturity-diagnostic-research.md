## Key Findings

- Maturity timing should be graded separately from refinance economics.
- The most useful diagnostic names one controlling constraint: proceeds, time, cash flow, value, lender appetite, rollover, reserve need, sponsor liquidity, or servicing path.
- A 30/60/90 day plan is required once a loan is inside the active refinance window.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Use four urgency bands: early watch, active refi window, urgent maturity, and critical/default risk.
- Require DSCR, debt yield, LTV, and gap snapshot where data allows.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- Fed/OCC macro-risk signals inform caution; deal-level lender quotes and loan documents control decisions.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Extension options can fail if DSCR, debt yield, leasing, or value tests are not met.
- Sponsor liquidity can be the binding issue even when property value appears adequate.

## Open Questions

- Actual payoff and extension tests require loan documents and lender statement.
