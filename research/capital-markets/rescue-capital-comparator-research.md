## Key Findings

- Rescue capital must be judged by exit feasibility, not only proceeds.
- Preferred equity can solve senior debt constraints but may impose governance and dilution triggers.
- Mezzanine, preferred equity, and common JV equity have different remedies and consent paths.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require scorecard by certainty, time, amount, cash pay, total cost, consent, control, dilution, and exit.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- Use legal sources for issue spotting; live term sheets control economics.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Preferred return accrues beyond realistic exit value.
- Rescue capital creates control loss before stabilization.

## Open Questions

- Senior-lender recognition and intercreditor terms are deal-specific.
