## Key Findings

- Office tenant credit review must combine credit, space dependence, industry exposure, lease term, and replacement difficulty.
- Public company or rated parent support can improve credit confidence, but only if the lease has a guaranty or direct obligation.
- Sublease activity can indicate tenant overcapacity even before default.
- Tenant concentration drives lender and buyer risk.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Measure exposure by RSF and rent share.
- Separate operating tenant, parent, and guarantor.
- Use a qualitative watchlist when financial data is unavailable.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- Strong market demand does not offset weak tenant credit.
- Public parent credit does not support a lease unless the parent is liable.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Coworking tenants require extra scrutiny due to flexible end-user demand.
- Government and medical tenants may be durable but lease terms can be specialized.
- Tech and AI-related tenants can expand quickly but also carry volatility.

## Open Questions

- Private tenant financials and space utilization usually require borrower or tenant cooperation.
