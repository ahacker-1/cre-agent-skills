## Shared Source Themes

- National office recovery is real but uneven: prime and highly amenitized assets are outperforming commodity vacancy.
- Leasing economics must be evaluated on net effective rent and cash flow after TI, LC, free rent, and downtime.
- Office financeability depends on tenant durability, rollover, and reserve sizing, not just headline LTV.
- Office lease abstraction must focus on base-year recoveries, BOMA measurement, work letters, and tenant control rights.
