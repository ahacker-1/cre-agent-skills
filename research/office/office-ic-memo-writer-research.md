## Key Findings

- Office IC memos must distinguish in-place income from lease-up thesis.
- The central question is not only cap rate; it is whether the building can win tenants at economics that support the basis and capital stack.
- Leasing costs, rollover, tenant concentration, and lender structure should appear in the recommendation.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require GO / CONDITIONAL GO / NO-GO recommendation.
- Require market, rent roll, lease, tenant, TI/LC, underwriting, and financing sections.
- Require risk table with mitigants or conditions.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- Positive market recovery sources and negative debt stress sources can both be true. The memo resolves this by asset quality and execution risk.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- A memo should not recommend GO if financing, lease documents, major rollover, or TI/LC remains unresolved.
- Conversion downside must be supported by feasibility.

## Open Questions

- Committee-specific return thresholds and approval conditions are sponsor-specific.
