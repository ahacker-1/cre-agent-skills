# Office Financing Fit Research

## Purpose

- Supports `skills/office/office-financing-fit.md`
- Intended users: teams matching office assets to realistic lender lanes and debt structures

## U.S.-Only Assumptions

- Geography: United States
- Deal type: office acquisition debt, refinancing, bridge, and rescue capital
- Financing assumptions: live lender feedback controls over static ranges

## Key Findings

- High-quality stabilized office is financeable, but weak or transitional office requires structure and reserves.
- LTV is not enough; DSCR, debt yield, tenant rollover, and leasing-cost reserves often control proceeds.
- Regulatory and CMBS delinquency data support conservative office debt sizing.
- Different lender lanes fit different asset profiles.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require in-place and stabilized DSCR/debt yield.
- Require lender-lane fit table.
- Require reserve and structure recommendations.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- Improving investment volume and LTVs do not imply broad lender appetite. Treat lender recovery as quality-selective.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Active repositioning may need bridge debt rather than CMBS.
- A refinance thesis dependent on lower rates is not a sufficient exit plan.
- Sponsor liquidity can be more important than paper value when TI/LC is large.

## Open Questions

- Live spread, reserve, recourse, and covenant terms require current quotes.
