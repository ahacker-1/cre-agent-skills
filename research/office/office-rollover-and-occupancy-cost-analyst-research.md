## Key Findings

- Renewal probability depends on business need, space utilization, rent-to-market, build-out friction, and tenant credit.
- Rollover analysis must include downtime, TI, LC, and free rent.
- Occupancy-cost pressure is often qualitative unless tenant financial data is supplied.
- Market-level flight-to-quality can improve prime renewal chances but does not eliminate tenant downsizing risk.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require renewal vs re-let economics for material tenants.
- Use RSF and rent share for rollover exposure.
- Treat tenant occupancy cost as qualitative unless revenue or headcount data is provided.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- Market reports show improving demand, but individual tenant behavior can still be negative. Tenant-specific evidence controls.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Above-market tenants may renew only with rent reset or contraction.
- Signed leases may not commence until after landlord work is complete.
- Free rent and downtime can create debt-service stress before stabilized NOI appears.

## Open Questions

- Tenant utilization and headcount are usually not public.
