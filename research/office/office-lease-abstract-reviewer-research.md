## Key Findings

- Office lease abstraction must cover economics, recoveries, measurement, work letters, and options.
- Base-year and expense-stop language can materially change NOI.
- Work letters affect capital needs and rent commencement.
- Assignment, sublease, contraction, renewal, and termination rights affect valuation and financing.

## Benchmark and Formula Decisions

- Require a lease-stack inventory before analysis.
- Require issue severity for each diligence concern.
- Treat missing work letters and amendments as material gaps.

## Conflicting Source Resolution

- Model forms and commentary are useful, but the signed lease stack controls.

## Edge Cases and Red Flags

- Estoppels can reveal differences from abstracts.
- Government leases may use special measurement or termination provisions.
- Side letters can change economics and are often missing from initial data rooms.

## Open Questions

- State law and enforceability require legal review.
