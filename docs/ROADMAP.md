# Roadmap

This roadmap reflects the intended direction of `cre-agent-skills` as a broader U.S. commercial real estate skills library.

## Current State

- Multifamily acquisitions toolkit
- Shared CRE legal, closing, and document-ingestion tools
- Research-backed Industrial v1 expansion
- Seller-side Brokerage Investment Sales v1 pack
- Post-acquisition Asset Management v1 pack
- Research-backed Office v1 expansion
- Cross-property Capital Markets v1 pack for maturities, refinance gaps, workouts, rescue capital, and recap decisions
- Research-backed Retail v1 expansion for trade area, tenant mix, co-tenancy, CAM recovery, underwriting, and financing fit
- Lender / Credit v1 pack - the first credit-side pack, covering screening and sizing, sponsor and appraisal review, credit memos, covenant and watchlist monitoring, annual review and risk rating, problem loans, and portfolio stress testing
- Development and Construction v1 pack for site and entitlement screening, budgets and yield on cost, construction loan sizing, draw and cost-to-complete review, GC contracts, schedule risk, lease-up pro formas, and development IC memos
