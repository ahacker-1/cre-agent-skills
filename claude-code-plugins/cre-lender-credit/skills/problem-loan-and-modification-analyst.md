# Problem Loan and Modification Analyst

Classify a deteriorating U.S. commercial real estate loan, name the source of impairment, compare every resolution path on recovery, timing, cost, and accounting consequence, and produce a lender action plan with milestones and downgrade triggers.

---

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when a CRE credit has moved past monitoring: coverage has failed, the maturity cannot be refinanced at par, the borrower has asked for relief, the loan has been downgraded, or the file has been handed to special assets. It answers five questions in order: what grade the credit carries today, what is actually broken, what the real paths out are, which one produces the best risk-adjusted recovery, and what the institution owes in classification, accrual, valuation, and allowance treatment once the path is chosen. It is written for bank and credit union special assets officers, credit officers, portfolio managers, and loan review staff; debt fund, life company, and CMBS credit staff can use the path comparison, but the classification, nonaccrual, and allowance sections follow the interagency supervisory framework that governs insured depositories and do not apply to them.

This is educational decision support, not legal, tax, investment, accounting, or financing advice. Classification, nonaccrual, charge-off, allowance, and regulatory reporting conclusions belong to the institution and its regulator, and foreclosure, receivership, redemption, and deficiency law is state-specific and belongs to counsel.

---

## What You'll Need to Provide

- Loan terms and status: current balance, rate and index, maturity, amortization, remaining interest-only, extension options and conditions, days past due, accrual status, prior modifications and dates, any prior charge-off, and the current risk rating with its assignment date
- Property: type and subtype, market, occupancy, trailing 12 and trailing 3 operating statements, current rent roll with expirations, budget, and deferred maintenance or capital needs
- Collateral value: most recent appraisal or evaluation with effective date and value type (as-is, as-completed, as-stabilized), and any known change in market or property condition since
- Sponsor and guarantors: current financial statements, verified liquidity, global cash flow, contingent liabilities to all lenders, guarantee form and enforceability, and prior performance under stress
- Structure and documents: lien position, cash management status, reserve balances, recourse and carve-out triggers, covenants and defaults declared or waived, participation or intercreditor terms
- The borrower's ask, if one has been made, and what is offered in exchange
- Institutional parameters: classification, nonaccrual, charge-off, and allowance policy; rating definitions and downgrade triggers; delegated workout authority; and any concentration or OREO limit that constrains a path

If value, sponsor financials, or institutional policy are not supplied, run the analysis on stated assumptions, label them, and withhold the classification and accrual conclusions rather than inventing them.

---

## Mission

Turn a deteriorating credit into a defensible, documented decision: a supportable classification and accrual position, a named source of impairment, a side-by-side comparison of resolution paths on net recovery and time, a chosen path with the accounting and reporting consequences it carries, and an action plan whose milestones and downgrade triggers can be monitored and reported.

## Strategy

### Step 1: Build the Facts and Name the Source of Impairment

Restate the credit, then diagnose. Distinguish the presenting symptom from the cause, because the cause determines which paths are real.

| Source of impairment | Diagnostic evidence | What it implies |
|---|---|---|
| Maturity / refinance gap | Loan performing, balance exceeds what a market-terms refinance supports at current rates and cap rates | Time and amortization may fix it; test the exit before extending |
| Cash flow shortfall | DSCR below 1.0x on actual debt service, rent roll erosion, expense reset (taxes, insurance) | Ask whether it is temporary and self-curing or structural |
| Rollover and re-leasing | Major expirations, negative absorption, cost to re-tenant exceeds available reserves and sponsor funds | Capital problem before it is a cash flow problem |
| Value impairment | Cap rate expansion or NOI decline puts value below balance | On its own, not a downgrade; only matters with a weakness |
| Construction or business plan failure | Budget overrun, stalled construction, absorption far behind pro forma, depleted interest reserve | The heaviest case; treat serial extensions as deterioration |
| Sponsor failure | Liquidity exhausted, obligations to other lenders, litigation, bankruptcy, non-cooperation | Changes every path; recourse and guarantee analysis becomes central |
| Structural or documentation | Lien, insurance, escrow, or reporting defects; covenant defaults never cured or waived | Fix the file before negotiating anything |

Then state where the payments have been coming from. A loan current only because an interest reserve is funding it, because a guarantor is covering shortfalls, or because a serial extension keeps resetting maturity is not a performing loan. Supervisory guidance treats an interest reserve or serial extension masking a stalled project, and additional advances used to service an existing loan without credible repayment support, as classification triggers on their own.

### Step 2: Refresh the Collateral Value for the Path You Are Actually Considering

Nothing downstream is defensible on a stale or wrong-type value. The interagency policy statement directs the institution to consider a new appraisal or evaluation on material deterioration in project performance, on geographic market and property type conditions, on variances between actual conditions and the original appraisal assumptions, on changes in project specifications, on loss of a significant lease or take-out commitment, and on increases in pre-sale fallout. The 2010 Interagency Appraisal and Evaluation Guidelines add validity factors: passage of time, market volatility, changes in financing availability, natural disasters, supply changes, improvements or deterioration to the subject or competing properties, changes in cap rate and lease-term assumptions, zoning or technology changes, and environmental contamination.

Whether a new appraisal is required is a regulatory test, not a preference. Under 12 CFR 34.43 a CRE transaction at $500,000 or less is exempt, as is a business loan of $1 million or less not dependent on real estate as the primary repayment source. A renewal or refinancing is exempt where either there has been no obvious and material change in market conditions or physical aspects of the property threatening collateral protection, or there is no advancement of new monies beyond funds necessary to cover reasonable closing costs. Where new money is advanced, go back to the appraisal regulation. An institution-prepared evaluation may substitute where it appropriately updates the original appraisal's assumptions to current market conditions and produces a reasonable fair value estimate.

Match the value conclusion to the plan, which is the most common error here. A work-with-the-borrower plan aimed at stabilization may use the as-stabilized market value for credit risk grading once the appraisal's assumptions and conclusions are confirmed reasonable; a plan to foreclose requires the as-is fair value less costs to sell in the property's current condition for financial reporting. Costs to sell come from the appraisal, broker input, or institutional experience, and are stated rather than assumed away. Appraisal assumptions from a qualified appraiser get reasonable deference and marginal differences from norms are not challenged, but where facts materially deviate from the assumptions the estimated value may be adjusted for credit analysis and classification where the adjustment is supportable and not a worst case. Detailed review belongs to [Appraisal and Valuation Reviewer](skills/lender-credit/appraisal-and-valuation-reviewer.md).

### Step 3: Set the Classification and the Accrual Position

Grade the credit on well-defined weaknesses, using the definitions and split-rating mechanics in [Regulatory Risk Rating and Classification](knowledge/regulatory-risk-rating-and-classification.md). Two supervisory rules cut in opposite directions: a performing loan is not adversely classified or charged off solely because collateral value fell below the balance, and a loan that is current or fully covered by collateral is still classified when well-defined weaknesses jeopardize repayment.

Examiner-named well-defined weaknesses in CRE terms: excess similar product under construction; analysis that does not reflect current and reasonably anticipated market conditions; rent concessions pushing cash flow below expectations; concessions on tenant space, moving expenses, and lease buyouts; slow leasing that protracts repayment; delinquent lease payments from major tenants; tax arrearages; minimal or no borrower equity; additional advances used to service the existing loan without credible repayment support; and renewals or extensions lacking credible repayment support and a reasonable repayment schedule.

Where repayment depends on the sale of the collateral, apply the split:

| Portion of balance | Classification |
|---|---|
| Adequately secured by fair value of the real estate less costs to sell | Generally no worse than substandard |
| Excess over that amount | Loss |
| Excess where full loss may be mitigated by a pending event, or the loss amount cannot reasonably be determined | Doubtful, used infrequently and reassessed promptly |

Then set accrual. Interest is not accrued where the asset is maintained on a cash basis because of borrower deterioration, where payment in full of principal or interest is not expected, or where principal or interest has been in default 90 days or more, unless the asset is both well secured and in the process of collection. Well secured means collateral with realizable value sufficient to discharge the debt including accrued interest, or the guarantee of a financially responsible party. In process of collection means collection proceeding in due course through legal action or, in appropriate circumstances, non-legal efforts reasonably expected to restore the loan to current in the near future. A waiver of interest or principal generally puts a commercial loan on nonaccrual; a deferral without waiver is a judgment call on whether full payment is still expected. A guarantee improves the rating only where the guarantor has demonstrated ability, willingness, and incentive and the guarantee is enforceable; hand that work to [Sponsor and Guarantor Analyst](skills/lender-credit/sponsor-and-guarantor-analyst.md).

### Step 4: Build the Path Menu and Price Each One

Build every path the documents, the state, and the counterparty actually allow, then compare them on the same basis. Compare net present value of expected recovery, not gross recovery, because time to resolution differs by years across this menu and carrying cost, workout expense, and foregone interest differ with it.

| Path | When it fits | Lender protections to require | Principal cost or risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short-term accommodation | Temporary, self-curing shortfall, before a true workout | Current financials, defined end date, no new money, reporting | Delay if the problem is structural, not temporary |
| Extension or modification | Credible plan, sponsor support, exit visible within the new term | Curtailment or re-margin, added recourse or guaranty, reserve funding, tighter covenants, performance milestones | Reportable modification; serial extensions read as deterioration |
| Forbearance | Lender wants standstill while a sale, refinance, or capital event closes | Reaffirmation of debt, waiver of defenses, cash management, hard deadline, cooperation covenants | No cure if the underlying event fails to close |
| A/B note split | Balance exceeds collateral value but a market-terms loan is supportable | Note A sized to fair value at market terms; Note B charged off, not forgiven; performance period before Note A accrues | Note B is a charge-off in the period taken |
| Discounted payoff | Borrower or a third party can fund a payoff above net foreclosure recovery | Funds verified, release documented, deficiency and carve-out treatment addressed | Immediate loss recognition; sets a precedent in the portfolio |
| Note sale | Faster and cleaner than foreclosure, or the institution wants the exposure off the book | Bid process documented, loan file complete, representations limited | Price discovery risk; loss recognized on sale |
| Receivership | Property mismanaged, cash diverted, or asset needs stabilizing before sale | Consent-to-receiver provision, court appointment, receiver reporting and budget | State-specific availability, cost, and powers; timeline uncertainty |
| Deed in lieu | Cooperative borrower, clean title, no junior liens or environmental exposure | Title search, estoppel from junior lienholders, environmental review, release terms | Takes title with existing liens and liabilities; OREO consequences |
| Foreclosure | No cooperative path, or gross recovery clearly exceeds the alternatives net of time and cost | Counsel-run timeline, redemption and deficiency analysis, bid strategy | Longest and costliest path; OREO carrying and holding limits |

For each path, state gross recovery, timeline in months, workout expense (legal, appraisal and evaluation fees, consulting, receiver, liquidation costs), carrying cost including interest foregone during nonaccrual, and net present value. Workout expenses must be captured and accounted for properly and not charged to the allowance. Screen feasibility before pricing: participation or intercreditor voting rights, junior liens, environmental exposure, bankruptcy risk, guarantor cooperation, and whether the institution has the OREO capacity and workout staffing to carry it. The counterparty view of the same event is [Extension / Workout Strategy Builder](skills/capital-markets/extension-workout-strategy-builder.md), [Debt Maturity Diagnostic](skills/capital-markets/debt-maturity-diagnostic.md), and [Lender Update Package Builder](skills/capital-markets/lender-update-package-builder.md); for securitized loans see [CMBS Servicing and Default Playbook](knowledge/cmbs-servicing-and-default-playbook.md) and [CMBS / Special Servicing Readiness Reviewer](skills/capital-markets/cmbs-special-servicing-readiness-reviewer.md). Cash flow math is not restated here; use [Underwriting Calculations](knowledge/underwriting-calc.md).

### Step 5: State the Accounting and Reporting Consequences of the Chosen Path

- **Modification reporting.** Under ASC 326 with ASU 2022-02 adopted, the reportable event is a modification to a borrower experiencing financial difficulty in one or more of four forms: principal forgiveness, an interest rate reduction, an other-than-insignificant payment delay, and a term extension. Financial difficulty indicators include default or foreseeable default absent modification, bankruptcy, going-concern doubt, insufficient cash flow to service the debt, and inability to obtain funds elsewhere at a market rate. A contractually current loan can still produce a reportable modification, and waiting for the maturity date to grant a below-market renewal does not exempt it. A short-term delay is not automatically insignificant: weigh the delayed amount against unpaid principal or collateral value and the delay period against payment frequency, original contractual maturity, or original expected duration, considering the cumulative effect of modifications in the prior 12 months. Institutions that have not adopted ASC 326 or ASU 2022-02 continue reporting troubled debt restructurings under the applicable instructions.
- **Nonaccrual and return to accrual.** A restructured loan reasonably assured of repayment and performance on prudent modified terms need not go to nonaccrual, provided the restructuring and any charge-off are supported by a current, well-documented credit evaluation; otherwise it stays or goes on nonaccrual. Return to accrual requires sustained repayment performance for a reasonable period, generally a minimum of six months of cash or cash-equivalent payments, with pre-restructuring performance countable. No accounting or regulatory source sets a hard minimum, so facts and circumstances govern; the narrow exception is a modification coinciding with an event showing significant improvement in repayment ability, such as substantial new leases in a troubled project, significant new revenue sources, or significant new equity from a source not financed by the institution.
- **A/B note conditions.** Note A may return to accrual only where the partial charge-off is supported by a good faith credit evaluation and recorded before or at the modification, ultimate collectibility of all amounts contractually due on Note A is not in doubt, and satisfactory payment performance exists immediately before or after the modification. If any condition fails, or the modification lacks economic substance, the loan remains nonaccrual. Note B is classified loss and charged off; a charged-off amount is never rebooked.
- **Allowance.** Expected credit losses on modified loans are estimated under the same CECL methodology as every other loan, collectively unless risk characteristics have diverged. A loan is collateral-dependent where repayment is expected substantially through operation or sale of the collateral and the borrower is experiencing financial difficulty at the reporting date; for regulatory reporting the ACL on such a loan uses the collateral's fair value less estimated costs to sell where applicable, even where foreclosure is not probable, measured at the reporting date without adjusting for expected future value changes. Classification and allowance are related but not equivalent conclusions.
- **Foreclosure and OREO.** On receipt of the real estate, OREO is recorded at fair value less estimated costs to sell, the remaining loan balance is derecognized, and the excess of loan cost basis over that amount is a loss charged to the allowance. That amount becomes the new cost basis; later holding-period declines run through a property-specific valuation allowance, and unallocated valuation allowances are not acceptable. For collateral other than residential real estate, the loan is reclassified at the earlier of physical possession or legal title, where physical possession means actual custody or control.

### Step 6: Write the Action Plan, Milestones, and Downgrade Triggers

The 2023 interagency policy statement says an institution implementing a prudent workout will not be criticized for it even where the restructured loan carries weaknesses producing an adverse classification. The condition is a documented file. Confirm all six workout-program elements are satisfied before recommending anything, using the same enumeration as [Regulatory Risk Rating and Classification](knowledge/regulatory-risk-rating-and-classification.md): a well-conceived, prudent, documented workout plan for the loan, which itself carries updated and comprehensive financial information on the borrower, the real estate project, and all guarantors and sponsors, current valuations of the collateral supporting both the loan and the workout plan, structure and covenants appropriate to the plan, and appropriate legal analysis and documentation for every change to loan terms or collateral with lien perfection confirmed; a global debt service coverage analysis with realistic cash flow projections and the availability, continuity, and accessibility of repayment sources; analysis of available guarantor cash flow including obligations to all other lenders; demonstrated willingness and ability to monitor borrower and guarantor performance under the workout terms; an internal risk rating that accurately and consistently reflects the risk in the workout arrangement; and a GAAP allowance methodology recognizing losses timely through provision and charge-off, including an allowance on related off-balance-sheet exposures.

Then write the plan: the structure (term, rate, amortization, curtailment, re-margining, reserves, added recourse or guaranty, cash management, reporting), the milestones with dates and the party responsible, the specific triggers for a further downgrade or for moving to the fallback path, and the fallback itself. Two structural decisions require an explicit written rationale rather than a default: re-amortizing a maturing balance back over the original period, which lowers the payment and diverts cash flow from the institution to the borrower, and any amortization schedule generous enough to suit a slow-but-selling project when the project is in fact stalled with no guarantor able to supplement principal. Feed the covenants and reporting requirements to [Covenant Compliance and Watchlist Monitor](skills/lender-credit/covenant-compliance-and-watchlist-monitor.md), the rating conclusion to [Annual Loan Review and Risk Rating](skills/lender-credit/annual-loan-review-and-risk-rating.md), the approval write-up to [Credit Memo Writer](skills/lender-credit/credit-memo-writer.md), and the portfolio consequence to [CRE Portfolio Concentration and Stress Tester](skills/lender-credit/cre-portfolio-concentration-and-stress-tester.md). A request that is really new money on a troubled credit is screened, not assumed; route it to [Loan Request Screening and Sizing](skills/lender-credit/loan-request-screening-and-sizing.md).

## Quality Checks

- The source of impairment is named, and it is a cause rather than a symptom
- The value used carries a type, an effective date, and a stated match to the recommended path; an as-stabilized value never supports a liquidation case, and an as-is liquidation value never supports a funded lease-up case
- The classification is supported by named well-defined weaknesses, not by a collateral value decline alone and not waived away by a current payment record
- The collateral-dependent split reconciles: secured portion plus excess equals the balance, and doubtful is used only for a pending event or an indeterminate loss amount
- The accrual conclusion names the test that drove it, and a waiver of interest or principal is distinguished from a deferral
- Every path is screened for feasibility (documents, state law, junior liens, participations, environmental, counterparty cooperation) before it is priced, and priced on net present value with a stated source behind each recovery, timing, and cost assumption
- The reportable modification test is run against all four forms and the insignificant-delay factors, not assumed from the label on the document, and workout expenses are not charged to the allowance
- The six workout-program elements are each marked present or gapped with an owner, and milestones carry dates, owners, evidence, and measurable downgrade triggers

## Red Flags & Dealbreakers

- The loan is contractually current only because an interest reserve, a serial extension, or a guarantor advance is funding the payments while the project has stalled, or new money is being advanced to service the existing loan without credible support for full repayment
- The recommended path is an extension whose exit depends on cap rate compression or rate relief rather than amortization, cash flow growth, or a funded capital event
- A below-market renewal is timed to the maturity date to avoid characterizing it as a modification, or a modification of a current loan is assumed unreportable
- The classification rests on an appraisal more than a re-order trigger old, on a value type that does not match the plan, or on a borrower-supplied value
- A guarantee carries the rating with no evidence of the guarantor's financial ability, willingness, and incentive, or the guarantor is litigating or protracting renegotiation
- Doubtful has persisted across quarters without the pending event resolving, or special mention is being used to avoid the substandard call
- A charged-off amount is rebooked when the borrower recovers, or Note A returns to accrual with no performance period and no coinciding improvement event
- Foreclosure is recommended without an environmental review, a title and junior lien search, or a state-specific redemption and deficiency analysis, or deed in lieu is recommended where junior liens or environmental exposure would transfer with title
- A path is recommended that the participation, intercreditor, or servicing documents do not permit the institution to elect unilaterally
- Workout costs are charged to the allowance, or holding-period OREO declines sit in an unallocated valuation allowance, or the plan has no fallback, no measurable downgrade trigger, and no date by which the institution stops negotiating

## When Data is Missing

- No current appraisal or evaluation: state which re-order trigger is met, treat the existing value as stale, present the classification split across a value range rather than a point, and make the ordered and reviewed valuation the first milestone. Where costs to sell are also unknown, show the secured portion at a range and label the fair-value-less-costs-to-sell figure preliminary
- No current borrower or guarantor financials: the workout file is incomplete by definition; do not credit guarantor support in the rating, and make the financial statement request a dated milestone with a downgrade trigger attached
- No institutional classification, charge-off, or allowance policy: present the supervisory framework and the supportable range, and withhold the final grade, accrual position, and charge-off amount
- No recovery, timing, or cost history for the resolution paths: no primary source publishes these by path, so require institutional experience or counsel estimates, label every figure as an assumption, and show the comparison as a sensitivity rather than a point answer
- No state-law analysis of foreclosure timing, redemption, receivership availability, or deficiency exposure: mark those paths as unpriced pending counsel rather than estimating a national average
- No participation, intercreditor, or servicing documents: mark path feasibility as unconfirmed and do not recommend a path that may require consent the institution does not control

## Confidence Scoring

| Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| HIGH | Current appraisal or evaluation matched to the plan, current borrower and guarantor financials with verified liquidity and contingent liabilities, complete loan and lien documentation, institutional classification and allowance policy known, and path feasibility confirmed against documents and counsel |
| MEDIUM | Core operating and loan data available but one of a current valuation, guarantor financials, or a state-law timeline is missing or stale; classification presented across a value range and one or more paths priced on stated assumptions |
| LOW | Stale or borrower-supplied value, no sponsor financials, unknown institutional policy, or unconfirmed path feasibility; output is a diagnosis and a framework for the decision, not a rating, accrual, or charge-off conclusion |
